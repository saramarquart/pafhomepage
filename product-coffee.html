Coffee Alternative

The coffee experience — taste and ritual — without the bean, and without a climate-exposed, increasingly scarce supply chain.

Overview

Coffee, engineered for the formats the market sells in.

Coffee is under pressure from climate and price. Our Coffee Alternative recreates the taste and ritual of coffee without the bean — engineered for the formats the market actually sells in.

From soluble and 3-in-1 mixes to hybrid blends and ready-to-drink, it drops into existing coffee formats and processes.

Formats

Soluble

Soluble (instant)

A bean-free instant coffee for jars, sticks and sachets.

3-in-1

3-in-1 mixes
