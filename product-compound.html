Compound Solutions

Blend Cocoa Booster with cocoa powder in liquid and compound applications — at 50/50, 30/70, or all the way to a full 100% cocoa replacement.

Get in touch sales@forplaneta.com

Overview

Dial cocoa down to your target.

Compound and other liquid applications are where cocoa cost hits hardest. Compound Solutions blend Cocoa Booster — which contains no cocoa — with cocoa powder, so you set the cocoa content to your target.

Go 50/50, 30/70, or all the way to a full 100% cocoa replacement — without losing the indulgent taste, and drop-in for your existing liquid and compound lines.

Built for your line

50 / 50

50/50 blend

Half cocoa powder, half Cocoa Booster — a balanced first step into cocoa reduction, keeping the indulgent taste.

30 / 70

30/70 blend

A deeper cut: 30% cocoa powder, 70% Cocoa Booster, for a firmer hedge on cocoa cost.

100%

100% replacement

Fully cocoa-free: Cocoa Booster replaces the cocoa powder entirely in the application.
