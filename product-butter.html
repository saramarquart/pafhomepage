ChoViva Butter

Cocoa Butter Identical — precision-fermented from yeast. Not an equivalent or a substitute: compositionally identical to cocoa butter.

Overview

The smooth side of innovative food.

The next step after ChoViva: replacing cocoa butter and other tropical and specialty fats. Living yeast cultures can produce virtually any fat structure and composition — and our engineers have built a platform around them to deliver custom fats and oils.

ChoViva Butter outperforms today's confectionery plant-fat blends. It isn't a 'Cocoa Butter Equivalent' or 'Substitute' — it's compositionally identical to conventional cocoa butter, with the same snap, melt and velvety texture.

The technology

Platform

Yeast-derived food fats

Cultivated yeasts produce the full spectrum of fat and oil types found across plants and animals — steered by fermentation conditions to the exact fat you need.

Process

Precision fermentation

An ancient method brought to the next level: fine-tuned cultivation triggers the right biology to yield a fat compositionally identical to cocoa butter.
