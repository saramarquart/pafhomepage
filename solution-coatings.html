Coatings

Enrobing, moulding and compound coatings for bars, wafers and confectionery.

Talk to our team about your production line

Why this matters here

Matched to shade, snap and shine.

Coatings live or die on how closely they match the original in shade, snap and shine — get any of the three wrong and the finished product looks or feels wrong on shelf. That makes Innovation (an engineered taste-and-colour match) the leading benefit here, alongside Supply Chain Security; sustainability is a secondary, supporting line.

Our cocoa-free coatings and compounds are engineered to hold tempering, enrobing and moulding behaviour, so they drop into existing coating lines without retooling.
