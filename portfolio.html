The platform

One ingredient platform for the taste the world loves.

We rebuild the world's most-loved flavours from humble, abundant plant ingredients — grape seeds, plant fibre — fermented and roasted to match the taste and the colour of the originals, so they drop into your existing recipe and line. One platform, growing category by category: chocolate, cocoa, coffee, hazelnut and vanilla.

Why it works

Built to drop into your line — and your P&L.

Resilience matters, but it's one benefit among several. What decides a formulation is taste, colour, cost and supply — and the platform is built for all four.

Natural, high-quality ingredients

Every product starts from real, food-grade plant ingredients — abundant side-streams like grape seeds and plant fibre — nothing synthetic-tasting, nothing you can't put on a label.

Fermentation technology

Fermentation and roasting unlock the flavour of the originals without the vulnerable crop behind them — the taste, re-sourced.

Matches taste & colour

Engineered to match the flavour and the exact shade of what they replace — a drop-in, not a reformulation project.

Cost efficiency

Decoupled from volatile commodity markets. Stable, forecastable pricing at industrial scale — a lever on your cost base, not a hostage to the harvest.

Sustainability & resilience

A supply that doesn't hinge on threatened harvests, at a fraction of the land, water and carbon — resilience you can build a roadmap on.

One-stop platform

One partner across categories. As your needs grow, the platform grows with them — from a single ingredient to a full system.
