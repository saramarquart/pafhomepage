Company · People & Culture

Scientists, technologists and food people, together.

A team that treats taste as a craft and resilience as an engineering problem — for the bios, see our Leadership Team.

How we work

Curious, hands-on, and close to the product.

We're a small team solving a hard, physical problem — you can taste whether the science worked. That keeps us close to the product: scientists in the pilot plant, sales in the room with customers, and everyone within reach of a sample.

Our values

What we optimise for.

Patient science

Renewing an ingredient properly takes years, not a launch cycle — we plan for that horizon rather than rushing it.

Taste first

None of the rest matters if it doesn't taste right — every solution is judged against the original before anything else.

Built to ship

We build for industrial-scale production, not a pilot line — a result manufacturers can actually put on a line.

Growing team

Across Munich and Pilsen.

Our team has grown quickly across two sites, bringing together food science, engineering, sales and operations under one roof. See our current openings on the Careers page.
