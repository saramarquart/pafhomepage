Why now?

Two pressures. One recipe.

For manufacturers of cocoa-based foods, supply risk and changing demand land on the same formulations.

Volatility is a structural problem

Almost half of the world’s cocoa comes from Côte d’Ivoire and Ghana – weather and harvest risks hit the whole market. Cocoa Booster lets you reduce the cocoa powder share in your recipe, and with it your exposure. Sourced in the EU, volumes and prices can be secured long-term.

Fiber is the new formulation brief

Consumers on GLP-1 medication eat smaller portions and choose more deliberately, with a focus on nutrient density and fiber. Replacing part of the cocoa powder raises the fiber content of a recipe while keeping its taste and color.
