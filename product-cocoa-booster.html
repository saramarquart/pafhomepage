Cocoa Booster

Use less cocoa, keep the taste. A cocoa extender that hedges price volatility — with no reformulation drama.

Request samples

Overview

Take the volatility out of cocoa.

Cocoa Booster replaces a share of real cocoa with a fermented, sunflower-based extender — keeping the indulgent taste while cutting your exposure to cocoa's record prices and volatile supply.

It comes as a palette of shades — resembling natural, alkalized, highly-alkalized and black cocoa powder — so you can match the exact colour and taste your product needs, from a warm cocoa brown to a deep near-black.
