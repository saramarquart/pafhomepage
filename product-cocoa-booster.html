Overview

What it replaces, and why now.

Cocoa is under record price volatility and, increasingly, EUDR-driven due-diligence requirements on its supply chain (a compliance area for your legal team to confirm against your own sourcing — not a conclusion we make here). Cocoa Booster replaces a share of real cocoa with a fermented, sunflower-based extender, giving manufacturers a lever to dial down cocoa exposure without losing the taste.

How it works: it comes as a palette of shades — resembling natural, alkalized, highly-alkalized and black cocoa powder — so you can match the exact colour and taste your product needs, dropped in without reformulation drama.

Why Cocoa Booster

Three reasons manufacturers switch.

Supply Chain Security

Every kilo of real cocoa you replace is a kilo insulated from price volatility and cocoa-specific EUDR compliance exposure (flagged as a factor to weigh, not a legal determination).

Innovation

A shade-matched, drop-in extender — natural through black — so it moves at the speed of your recipe, not a reformulation project.

Sustainability

Sunflower-based rather than 100% tropical cocoa — a lower footprint per kilo, without a health or nutrition claim attached.
