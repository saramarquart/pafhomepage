About · Our Journey

One of the world's fastest-growing food-tech start-ups.

A success story made in Germany: visionary food technology for sustainable, 100% natural and future-oriented supply chains.

Planet A Foods has transformed from a small German food-technology start-up into a significant B2B player in the global food industry — all within just three years. What has fuelled us every step of the way is a strong belief that science holds the key to a better future. With our first "no-cocoa" ingredient ChoViva, we proved to the world that chocolatey pleasure and a significantly lower climate footprint no longer have to be at odds. And we are further intensifying our research to develop more solutions that will help transform the food industry.
