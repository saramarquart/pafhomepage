Co-creation space

Build it with us in the academy.

Co-development happens in our 500 m²+ pilot plant, laboratory and academy — we invite you in and develop side by side, from raw material to finished product, on real equipment: roll refining & conching, ball milling, coating and panning — for chocolate, compounds, cocoa drinks and coffee RTDs.

A full analytical lab (GC-MS, LC, rheometry) and a trained in-house sensory panel prove every recipe on the bench and on the palate.
