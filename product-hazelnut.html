Hazelnut Alternative & Booster

A hazelnut paste alternative — not the nut, the paste. Replace or extend hazelnut in spreads, fillings and pralines, insulated from a concentrated, weather-exposed crop.

Request samples

Overview

The roasted taste of hazelnut paste — protected from the harvest.

Hazelnut supply is concentrated and weather-exposed, and paste prices swing with every harvest. Our hazelnut paste alternative recreates the roasted taste and smooth body of hazelnut paste — to replace it outright or extend it, protecting both taste and margin.

It's built for paste applications, not whole-nut use: spreads, fillings, pralines and bakery.
