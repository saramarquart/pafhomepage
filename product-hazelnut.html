Overview

What it replaces, and why now.

Hazelnut supply is concentrated in a handful of growing regions and weather-exposed, so paste prices swing with every harvest. Our hazelnut paste alternative recreates the roasted taste and smooth body of hazelnut paste — to replace it outright or extend it, protecting both taste and margin.

How it works: it's built for paste applications, not whole-nut use — spreads, fillings, pralines and bakery — and drops into existing paste-handling lines.

Why Hazelnut Alternative & Booster

Three reasons manufacturers switch.

Supply Chain Security

Insulated from a concentrated, weather-exposed hazelnut crop and the paste-price swings that follow every harvest.

Innovation

Medium and dark roast profiles built for paste applications — a drop-in for spreads, fillings, pralines and bakery lines.
