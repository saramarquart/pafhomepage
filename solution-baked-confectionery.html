Baked Confectionery

Biscuits, cereal bars, snack bars and baked inclusions.

Talk to our team about your production line

Why this matters here

Where cocoa price hits margin hardest.

High-volume baked formats run on thin margins and high cocoa content — chips, chunks, coatings and fillings — which means cocoa-price exposure hits margin harder here than almost anywhere else in the portfolio. That's why this category leads with Cost Advantage and Supply Chain Security; sustainability is a secondary, supporting benefit rather than the headline.

Formats for inclusion, enrobing and lamination, engineered to your existing process — no line retooling required.

Proof point

+29.9% revenue uplift, in a real reformulation.

In a national retail case, a baked/cereal-bar recipe reformulated with ChoViva lifted revenue by up to +29.9% (Nielsen, Balisto/Cereola case) — a direct fit for this category, since the case itself is a baked, cereal-bar format.
