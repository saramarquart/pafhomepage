Functional Food

Protein and fortified bars, cereal and nutrition formats.

Talk to our team about your production line

Why this matters here

Indulgent taste, on a functional base.

Bars, cereal and fortified snacks pair a functional base (protein, fibre, fortification) with an indulgent top note — chocolate, cocoa or coffee — and that top note is exactly where commodity exposure and processing stability both bite hardest. That makes Supply Chain Security and Innovation (drop-in processing fit) the leading benefits here, with sustainability as a secondary line.

Our ingredients hold up under baking, extrusion and enrobing without disrupting the functional formulation underneath.

We make no health or nutrition claims about the finished product — the functional positioning is yours to make and substantiate.
