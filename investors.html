The growth case

Founded 2021. Already at industrial scale.

Planet A Foods was founded in 2021 to rebuild the world's most climate-exposed ingredients from resilient, natural inputs — starting with cocoa-free chocolate. Since then, the platform has scaled from a single product to a broader ingredient portfolio, running across two production sites and reaching manufacturers and brands worldwide. See the full history on our Journey page, and the current footprint on Global Footprint.
