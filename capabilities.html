Resilience by design

Two sites, one dependable supply.

Concentrating on a single facility concentrates risk. We produce across two locations so a disruption at one never becomes a disruption to you — the continuity a supplier of record has to be able to promise. With 30,000 metric tonnes of annual capacity, we support national listings and category-level volumes today, and our platforms are built to scale further as your programmes grow.

Food safety & quality

GFSI-certified, audit-ready

Our food-safety management is GFSI-certified — the benchmark global manufacturers and retailers require. Rigorous, documented, and ready for your quality and procurement teams.

Made to fit your line

Drop-in formats, real applications

Our ingredients are engineered for industrial processing and drop-in use — validated in real applications across sweet goods, snacks, bakery, confectionery, beverages and spreads, so integration is an upgrade, not a project.

Co-creation & Academy

Co-create with us — from raw material to finished product.

Beyond industrial scale, we run a 500 m²+ pilot plant, laboratory and academy — a space we open up to invite you in and build side by side. We take an idea from raw ingredients all the way to a finished, line-ready product, in the applications your market actually sells.

Cocoa drinks, coffee RTDs, compounds, chocolate, coatings and panned products — developed on real equipment, from roll refining & conching to ball milling — so what leaves the academy is ready to scale, not a slide.
