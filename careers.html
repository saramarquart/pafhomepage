Key facts

Planet A Foods at a glance

Founded in 2021, we make sustainable alternatives to commodity food ingredients. Our first product, ChoViva, is a cocoa-free chocolate alternative made from sunflower seeds, and it's already in more than 230 products across 10 countries.

How to join

Your steps to join our mission

Click through to see what to expect along the way.

  1. Step 01 of 5

    Introduction call and contact

    Phone call

    A first phone call to get to know each other: what drives you, what we are building and whether the role could be a match.

    Good to know

    Think about why our mission speaks to you. That is what we are most curious about.

  2. Step 02 of 5

    Role fit conversation

    Video call

    A deeper conversation with your future team lead about the role, your experience and how you like to work.

    Good to know

    Bring an example of work you're proud of, and your questions about the team.

  3. Step 03 of 5

    Technical session

    Case or task

    Depending on the role, a practical task or case discussion that shows how you approach the challenges we work on.

    Good to know

    There's rarely one right answer. We want to see how you think.

  4. Step 04 of 5

    Onsite experience

    In person

    Visit us in Munich or Pilsen, meet the people you'd work with and get a feel for our day-to-day.

    Good to know

    This is as much about you getting to know us. Ask anything.

  5. Step 05 of 5

    Reference calls

    Final step

    With your consent, we speak to one or two people you've worked with before we make our decision.

    Good to know

    Let your references know they may hear from us.
