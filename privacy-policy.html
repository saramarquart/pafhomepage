3. Legal Bases

Depending on the type and purpose of processing, the following legal bases of the GDPR may apply:

Legal basisDescription
Art. 6(1)(a) GDPRUnder this provision, the processing of personal data is permitted if you have given your explicit consent to it.
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPRUnder this provision, the processing of personal data is lawful if it is necessary for the performance of a contract to which you are a party or in order to take steps prior to entering into a contract.
Art. 6(1)(c) GDPRUnder this provision, the processing of personal data is lawful if it is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which we are subject.

4. Provision and Operation of the Website

Purpose of processing

When you visit our website, we process technically necessary data in order to display the website to you and to ensure its stability and security. In doing so, your device automatically transmits information to our server, which is temporarily stored in so-called server log files.

We process your IP address and other technical access data such as the date and time of access, the browser used and its version, the operating system and the page accessed.

Legal basis for processing

Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Our legitimate interest lies in the technically error-free provision, stability and security of our website.

5. Contacting Us

Purpose of processing

When you contact us, for example via the email addresses provided on our website or by phone, we process your information in order to handle your enquiry and in case of follow-up questions.

The contact links on our website (for example "Partner with us") open the email program on your own device. No data is transmitted to us via the website itself; we only receive your message once you send the email.

We process your name, your contact details and any other information arising from your enquiry.

Legal basis for processing

Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, insofar as your enquiry is aimed at the conclusion or performance of a contract.

Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Our legitimate interest lies in the effective handling of enquiries addressed to us.

6. Customer and Prospect Management ("CRM")

6.1 Customer Management and Business Development

Purpose of processing

We process personal data of prospects, customers and their contact persons in order to initiate, manage and maintain business relationships. For this purpose, we store and organise this data in Salesforce. This includes handling enquiries, preparing offers, supporting existing customers and documenting communication and the status of processing.

We process your master and contact data (e.g. name, company, position, business email address and phone number) as well as communication and contract data arising in the course of the business relationship.

Legal basis for processing

Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, insofar as the processing is necessary for the conclusion or performance of a contract or in order to take steps prior to entering into a contract.

Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Our legitimate interest lies in the efficient organisation and management of our business relationships and in the audit-proof documentation of communication.

6.2 Scheduling Appointments

Purpose of processing

You can contact us by email and arrange appointments with us, for example if you are interested in or have questions about our products.

We process your name, your business email address and the information you provide about your request and the desired appointment.

Legal basis for processing

Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, insofar as the appointment serves to take steps prior to entering into a contract.

Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Our legitimate interest lies in the efficient organisation and conduct of appointments and product demonstrations with prospects and customers.

7. Company Presence on Social Networks

Purpose of processing

We maintain presences on social networks such as LinkedIn in order to communicate with prospects and customers and to provide information about our services. If you visit one of our presences, the respective platform operator processes your personal data. We have no influence on this processing, which the platform operators carry out under their own responsibility.

We process the data that you share with us via the respective platform, for example when you interact with our content or contact us.

With regard to the processing of statistical usage data for our LinkedIn presence (so-called page or usage statistics), we and LinkedIn are joint controllers within the meaning of Art. 26 GDPR. The joint controllership concerns in particular the collection and analysis of usage data to create statistics, while the platform operator assumes primary responsibility for handling data subject rights and fulfilling information obligations. You can find the essential content of the joint controllership agreements in LinkedIn's privacy policy.

Legal basis for processing

Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Our legitimate interest lies in effective external presentation and communication with prospects and customers.

8. Marketing & Direct Advertising

We process your personal data in order to send you information about our products, offers and services, and we carry out measures for advertising purposes, customer retention and the optimisation of our offers. This is done, for example, in the form of emails and letters. In addition, we carry out market and opinion research, for example in the form of surveys. The legal basis for this is our legitimate interest in improving our products and services pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Where we obtain your separate consent for such measures, your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR is the legal basis.

Where we contact you for advertising purposes without an existing business relationship between us, in particular when advertising by email, we do so exclusively on the basis of your prior explicit consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. The same applies to advertising phone calls to consumers, which we only make with your prior explicit consent. Advertising calls to businesses are only made on the basis of presumed consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, for example via the method stated when you gave your consent or by sending us an informal message. The lawfulness of the processing carried out until the withdrawal remains unaffected.

If, on the other hand, you have already purchased goods or services from us, we use the email address provided in that context to send you advertising for our own similar products. The legal basis is our legitimate interest pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. In doing so, we rely on the exception for existing customers under Art. 13(2) of Directive 2002/58/EC (ePrivacy Directive) in conjunction with the respective national implementing laws; in Germany, this is Section 7(3) of the Act Against Unfair Competition (Gesetz gegen den unlauteren Wettbewerb, "UWG"). This use only takes place as long as you have not objected to it. We inform you of your right to object both when collecting your data and with every further advertising contact.

14. Data Transfers to Third Countries

Some of the recipients of your personal data named in the previous section are located in so-called third countries in which the level of data protection is not comparable to that in the EU. Third countries are all countries outside the European Economic Area. The European Economic Area comprises all countries of the European Union as well as the countries of the so-called European Free Trade Association, namely Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein.

Data transfers to such countries may take place, for example, if we use service providers located in a third country. Transfers of personal data to third countries take place on the basis of an adequacy decision of the European Commission. Where there is no adequacy decision of the European Commission for the respective third country, the transfer takes place on the basis of appropriate safeguards within the meaning of Art. 46(2) GDPR. Copies of these safeguards can be requested from the controller.

In addition, some recipients of your personal data based in the USA may be certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework ("DPF"). For such recipients, there is a limited adequacy decision for certain categories of data. To the extent of the data recipient's valid DPF certification, the personal data is transferred on the basis of this adequacy decision.

15. Storage Period

We only process personal data for as long as is necessary for the respective purpose of processing. As soon as the purpose no longer applies and no statutory retention obligation or other reason stated below prevents erasure, the data is deleted or anonymised.

The specific storage period is determined by the following criteria. If several criteria apply, the longest period is decisive. After it expires, the data is routinely deleted.

  • Purpose limitation: As long as we need the data to achieve the respective purpose of processing, for example to provide our services, to communicate with you or to perform a contract, we store it for the duration of this purpose.
  • Consent: We store data that we process on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) until you withdraw your consent, but no longer than until the purpose of processing no longer applies.
  • Contractual relationship: We generally store data that we process within the framework of a contractual relationship (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) until the contract has been fully processed, unless a statutory retention obligation or a legitimate interest requires longer storage.
  • Statutory retention obligations: Much data is subject to statutory retention obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with the respective statutory provisions) that prevent earlier erasure. Particularly relevant are retention periods under commercial law pursuant to the German Commercial Code (Handelsgesetzbuch, "HGB") and under tax law pursuant to the German Fiscal Code (Abgabenordnung, "AO"). Depending on the type of document, they generally amount to six to ten years from the end of the respective financial year. During these periods, the processing of the data is restricted and the data is kept exclusively to fulfil the statutory documentation obligation.
  • Legitimate interests: Storage beyond the aforementioned periods may take place insofar as we have a legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in retention and this outweighs the interests of the data subject worthy of protection. Such an interest may arise in particular from the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims for the duration of the applicable civil-law limitation periods under the German Civil Code (Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch, "BGB"), where the regular limitation period is three years (Section 195 BGB) and up to 30 years in certain cases (Section 197 BGB), as well as from preventing renewed contact after an objection or withdrawal, for example by keeping a blocklist (e.g. for recipients who have unsubscribed from advertising). In these cases, we restrict processing and delete the data as soon as the legitimate interest no longer applies.
  • Applications: We generally delete application documents and personal data of applicants no later than six months after the end of the application process, unless your application leads to employment and no legitimate interests prevent erasure. Retention for this period serves in particular the establishment, exercise or defence of claims under the German General Equal Treatment Act (Allgemeines Gleichbehandlungsgesetz, "AGG"). If your application leads to employment, we transfer the data required for the employment relationship to our HR management system and continue to process it in accordance with the privacy notice for employees. If you have consented to being included in our talent pool, we store your data on the basis of your consent until you withdraw it. If you do not withdraw your consent, we delete your data after 2 years of inactivity.

The specific deletion periods for individual processing activities are set out in our internal deletion and retention concept.

16. Data Subject Rights

You can exercise the rights listed below. Your requests to exercise data protection rights and our responses to them are retained for documentation purposes for a period of three years after the end of the year in which we responded to your request and, in individual cases, beyond that for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. The legal basis for this is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in protecting ourselves against claims or fines under Art. 82 and 83 GDPR and in fulfilling our accountability obligation under Art. 5 GDPR.

You have the right to request information from us about your stored personal data. This information covers whether Planet A Foods GmbH processes/stores personal data about you and, among other things, which data is involved and for what purposes this data is processed. Upon request, Planet A Foods GmbH will provide you with a copy of the data.

16.2 Right to Rectification, Art. 16 GDPR

You also have the right to request the correction of incorrectly stored data or the completion of incomplete data.

16.3 Right to Erasure, Art. 17 GDPR

You also have the right to request the erasure of your personal data, provided that the legal requirements for this are met. We are obliged to erase data, among other things, if your personal data is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or otherwise processed, if you have withdrawn consent you previously gave, or if the data has been processed unlawfully.

16.4 Right to Restriction of Processing, Art. 18 GDPR

Subject to the legal requirements, you have the right to restriction of the processing of your personal data.

16.5 Right to Data Portability, Art. 20 GDPR

You have the right to have the data that you have provided to us on the basis of your consent or an existing contractual relationship with you handed over to you in a machine-readable format or, at your choice, transferred to a third party.

16.6 Right to Object, Art. 21 GDPR

Right to object on grounds relating to your particular situation

Where we process your data for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority (Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR), or where the processing is based on the legitimate interests of Planet A Foods GmbH (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you have the right to object at any time to the processing of your personal data on grounds relating to your particular situation.

This also applies to profiling based on this provision within the meaning of Art. 4(4) GDPR. We will then stop the processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which override your interests in the processing being stopped.

Right to object to the processing of data for direct marketing purposes

In individual cases, we process your personal data for direct marketing purposes. You have the right to object at any time to the processing of personal data concerning you for the purpose of such marketing. This also applies to profiling to the extent that it is related to such direct marketing. If you object to processing for direct marketing purposes, we will no longer process your personal data for these purposes.
