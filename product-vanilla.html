Overview

What it replaces, and why now.

Vanilla is one of the most price-volatile ingredients in food, exposed to a narrow, weather-sensitive growing region. Our Vanilla Replacer delivers a rounded, dependable profile to replace or extend it — with consistency you can plan and forecast against.

How it works: clean to use and stable in cost, it drops into existing formulations and takes the guesswork out of one of your most unpredictable lines.

Why Vanilla Replacer

Three reasons manufacturers switch.

Supply Chain Security

A rounded, dependable profile that isn't exposed to a single, weather-sensitive growing region and its price swings.

Innovation

Clean, simple to use and drop-in — replace or extend, batch after batch, with a consistent profile.

Sustainability

A more forecastable, resilient alternative to natural vanilla, without a health or nutrition claim attached.
