Why we exist

We take the long view on taste.

Nature is changing, and the crops behind the world's favourite flavours feel it first. We don't meet that with alarm — we meet it with patience, science and a longer horizon, renewing each ingredient so it can be relied upon, quietly, for generations.

CocoaThe soul of chocolate — kept within reach.
CoffeeThe world's daily ritual — made dependable.
VanillaThe most beloved of flavours — made steady.
HazelnutThe heart of spreads and pralines — secured.

Rooted in nature

What nature has given us for centuries, we intend to keep giving.

Human by nature

The flavours that shaped human culture.

Taste is where people and nature meet — the small, daily pleasures that hold cultures together. Our work is quiet and singular: to make sure they last, for the generations who haven't tasted them yet.
