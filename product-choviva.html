Overview

We've reinvented chocolate for the next generation.

It tastes, looks and feels like chocolate. ChoViva is the world's leading chocolate alternative, made without cocoa, created for the food industry with all the characteristics of chocolate — from the snappy experience to the velvety mouthfeel and rich taste.

As versatile as the original, it's a real, future-oriented and cost-efficient alternative for mass-market products — with a stable supply chain and a far smaller carbon footprint than regular chocolate.
