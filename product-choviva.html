Overview

What it replaces, and why now.

Chocolate depends on cocoa — a crop exposed to record price volatility and, increasingly, EUDR-driven due-diligence requirements on the supply chain (a compliance area we'd flag for your legal team to confirm against your own sourcing, not a conclusion we make for you). ChoViva is a cocoa-free chocolate alternative made from sunflower seeds, built to give manufacturers an independent supply chain for the taste chocolate provides.

How it works: ChoViva is engineered to match chocolate's taste, snap and melt — a drop-in for existing lines, with no retooling needed. It tastes, looks and feels like chocolate, built for the food industry with the same processing characteristics.

Why ChoViva

Three reasons manufacturers switch.

Supply Chain Security

An independent, non-cocoa supply chain — insulated from cocoa price volatility and the EUDR compliance burden building around cocoa specifically (flagged here as a factor to weigh, not a legal determination).

Innovation

A drop-in ingredient engineered to match chocolate's taste, snap and melt — no line retooling, so it moves at the speed of your product roadmap, not your capex cycle.

Sustainability

Made from sunflower seeds rather than tropical cocoa — a materially lower Scope 3 footprint (~80% lower CO₂ vs. cocoa, Planet A Foods) without a health or nutrition claim attached.
