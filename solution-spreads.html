Overview

Roasted taste, resilient recipe.

Spreads ride on two of the most volatile inputs in food: hazelnut paste and cocoa. Our hazelnut paste alternative and cocoa-free ingredients keep the roasted taste and smooth, spoonable body while insulating the margin.
